## What this proves, for the reviewer

- **Sample entries are sanitized independently**, not all-or-nothing: one malformed entry in
  `sample` is dropped, the rest of the message (successors/predecessors/advisory fields) is
  unaffected, and the parser never throws on a malformed entry.
- **Advisory numerics (`size_estimate`, `confidence`) are either intact or fully absent** —
  never coerced to `0` or any other value. This is the crux of the ticket: a receiver must not
  be able to mistake a stripped-because-malformed field for a genuine `0`.
  `field in parsed === false` is what the new rows assert, not `parsed[field] === 0`.
- **`metadata` is all-or-nothing** per the `isPlainObject` gate in `makeSnapshotParser` — any
  non-plain-object value is dropped whole, a plain object passes through unchanged.

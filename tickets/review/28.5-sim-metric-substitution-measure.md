Measurement-only ticket. **Nothing shipped, by design** — no source or test file changed. The
entire product is the `## Measured substitution table` section now written into
`tickets/implement/28.6-sim-metric-guard-case.md`, which is what the reviewer should read.

## What was done

A throwaway driver (`test/simulation/scratch-metric-measure.ts`, since deleted) ran the routing
measurement from `simulation.routing.spec.ts` at `n: 1000`, all-edge, over capacities 24 / 28 / 32,
three times: once unmodified, once with `minDistance` in `src/ring/distance.ts` replaced by
`clockwiseDistance(a, b)`, once with it replaced by a byte-wise XOR. The best two caps were then
re-run across all four seeds (1 / 4242 / 99 / 20260820) for all three metrics.

## The result, in one paragraph

A usable gap exists at `capacity: 32`. The shipped metric measured 100% success on every seed with
p90 7–8 hops; clockwise-only measured 83–90% success at p90 17–21; XOR measured 60–77% success at
p90 5–7. The finding that changes the next ticket's shape is that **XOR's p90 is lower than the
shipped metric's, not higher** — p90 is taken over successful routes only, and XOR fails the hard
routes outright rather than lengthening them, so its surviving routes flatter the hop distribution.
A hop bound alone therefore catches clockwise-only and misses XOR entirely; the success floor is
what catches XOR. Recommendation handed to `28.6`: cap 32, assert both `success >= 0.95` and
`p90 <= 12`.

## What a reviewer should check

- **The numbers are reproducible.** The driver is deleted, so re-running means re-creating it. The
  recipe is fully stated in `28.6`'s table caption (config, seeds, route count, convergence window)
  and the measurement body is `measureRouting()` in `test/simulation.routing.spec.ts` verbatim with
  `capacity` added to the config. One cell costs ~5–7 s wall; a full three-metric four-seed sweep at
  two caps is ~2.5 minutes plus two hand edits to `distance.ts`.
- **`src/ring/distance.ts` is back at HEAD.** `git diff src/` was clean before handoff. This is
  worth an eyeball because the file was edited twice during the run; the reverts were made with
  `git checkout -- src/ring/distance.ts` against edits this ticket had made itself, and touched no
  other path.
- **The caveat is stated, not buried.** `minDistance` is not selector-only — the relevance sparsity
  model and the sim's `nearRadiusFor` read it too, so each substituted row changes eviction shape as
  well as hop choice. The table's caption says so. If a reader could still come away thinking these
  rows isolate the selector, the wording needs strengthening.

## Known gaps — stated, not papered over

- **Single-run cells, no repetition per (metric, cap, seed).** Every number is one deterministic
  run. Seed spread is the only variance estimate, and it covers four seeds at caps 28 and 32 only —
  cap 24 was measured at seed 4242 alone and should not be quoted as a range.
- **The recommended success floor of 0.95 has 5 points of margin on each side** (shipped 100%,
  worst substituted 90%). That is the tightest number in the recommendation. If `28.6` finds the
  shipped case dipping below 100% on a seed not tested here, the floor is the assertion to
  re-examine, not the hop bound (which has 50% headroom).
- **No test was run and none was added.** `yarn test` was not run, because no shipped file changed;
  the only edits made were to `distance.ts` (reverted) and a scratch file (deleted). `28.6` runs the
  full suite when it lands the guard.
- **The `capacity < 2m + 1` protection interaction was not investigated.** At `m: 8` the protection
  set is 17 ids and the caps tested are 24–32, so eviction has 7–15 unprotected slots to work with.
  That is above the floor, but the margin is small enough that the recommended cap of 32 should not
  be lowered without re-measuring.

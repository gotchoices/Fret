Phase 3 of 3 of `backoff-test-surface-migration` (phases 1–2 already landed: `backoff-test-helper`
added `ProbeBackoff` and the `backoffOf`/`setBackoffOf` test helper). This phase converts the last
five specs off raw `(svc as any).backoffMap` / `clearBackoff` casts and onto the helper, then closes
out the whole chain with a grep verification and a docs update.

## What changed

All five files now import `backoffOf` from `./helpers/backoff.js` and use `backoffOf(svc).factor(id)`
/ `backoffOf(svc).clear(id)` instead of reaching into `(svc as any).backoffMap` or calling a
`clearBackoff` cast:

- `dead-state.spec.ts` — 4 sites converted: two "no backoff" assertions (`factor(id) === 0`), one
  "backoff recorded" assertion (`factor(id) !== 0`), and one `clearBackoff(hop)` → `.clear(hop)`.
- `rpc.stream-errors.spec.ts` — 2 sites: the `unspace()` helper's `clearBackoff` call, and a
  "no backoff" assertion inside "records nothing about a peer when our own signal aborts mid-reply".
- `rpc.stream-caps-local-limit-scoring.spec.ts` — the local `backoffFor` helper now returns
  `backoffOf(svc).factor(id)` (typed `number` instead of `unknown`), and both of its two call sites
  now assert `.to.equal(0)` instead of `.to.equal(undefined)`.
- `service-lifecycle.spec.ts` — the `stop()`-clears-the-map case (a "no backoff recorded against a
  peer we never dialed" assertion) now reads `backoffOf(svc).factor(targetId) === 0` instead of
  pulling the map out and calling `.get()` on it. This was the one site flagged as needing judgement
  in the ticket — it wasn't a `.get(id)` read to begin with, it grabbed the whole map — and `factor()
  === 0` is the direct public-surface equivalent of "nothing retained for this id", which is exactly
  what `stop()` clearing `ProbeBackoff`'s internal state produces.
- `stabilize-concurrency.spec.ts` — the diagnostic capture object literal's `backoff:` field now
  reads `backoffOf(svc).factor(id)`.

No second cast was introduced anywhere — every site above reaches `ProbeBackoff` only through the
`backoffOf` helper's public surface (`.factor(id): number`, `.clear(id): void`).

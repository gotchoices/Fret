  | case | fixed fields | + metadata | total |

  | Edge at today's emission caps 6/6/6 | 1,969 | 4,108 | 6,077 |

  So the 11,575 / 3,371 / 6,301 figures the comments assert are all correct as written, and the
  headroom claim ("~4.8 KiB under 16 KiB") holds. 53 characters is the conservative peer-id length
  — a real Ed25519 id is 52 and the shorter key types are shorter still. One residual the
  measurement does *not* cover: `relevance`, `size_estimate` and `confidence` were measured at
  11-ish characters each, whereas a JSON double can serialize to ~23; at 8 sample entries that is
  under ~150 extra bytes, far inside the headroom. Worth one sentence in the findings, not a
  ticket.

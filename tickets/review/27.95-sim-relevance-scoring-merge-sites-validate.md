## The deliberate deviation from production (recorded in code, repeated here for the reviewer)

The sim's KDE is fed by gossip and re-scores an already-held entry on every merge; production
does neither (a gossiped peer is scored once at creation, distance never observed again). Reason:
a sim peer sees orders of magnitude fewer contact events than a real node, so proven-contact-only
feeding supplies at most ~`maxConnections` observations per peer — at `alpha = 0.03` occupancy
stays near 0, the ideal/density ratio stays above `sMax^(1/beta) ~= 2.63`, and every entry clamps
at `sMax = 1.8` and ties. Same degeneracy relevance ranking exists to escape, one layer down. The
`NOTE:` lives on `scoreMerge` with one-line pointers at each merge site — worth reading in place,
since it's the one piece of this change that isn't a straight port of production behavior.

- **No test asserts on a relevance *value* anywhere.** The change is exercised only indirectly —
  existing specs stay green under it. That's a floor (nothing regressed), not a proof the scoring
  math is correct. Measurement plus a capacity sweep (setting `capacity` in a spec to actually
  trigger eviction and see relevance-ranked victim selection) are deferred to the parent ticket
  `sim-relevance-scoring` — this pair (`-merge-sites` / `-merge-sites-validate`) only wired the
  scoring calls in, it didn't validate the scoring *outcomes*.
- `enforceCapacity`'s protection set (self + live members both sides, `2*max(2,m)+1` ids) landed
  in an *earlier* run, not this diff — but it interacts directly with the new scoring (protection
  outranks score at eviction time), so a reviewer should look at it even though it's outside the
  diff being reviewed here.
- The gossip-fed-KDE deviation above is a design decision, not a bug — but it's the one place this
  sim's behavior diverges from production on purpose, so worth a second pair of eyes on whether
  the reasoning holds.

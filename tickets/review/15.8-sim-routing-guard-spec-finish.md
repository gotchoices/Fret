## Established by direct measurement (do not re-derive)

The safety grep hits: `chooseNextHop` is imported at `test/simulation/fret-sim.ts:6` and called
at `:879`, so the prerequisite routing change landed.

**Finding (major): the fourth case in `simulation.routing.spec.ts` — "a route whose originator is
already the peer nearest the target still succeeds" — is vacuous with respect to the rule it
claims to pin.** Its comment says the case exists because "without that split, a route whose
source is itself the nearest peer has no eligible first hop and fails". Measured: all 20 of its
routes complete at **hop 0**, and the selector is never called at all.

Why: `FretSimulation.addPeer` (`test/simulation/fret-sim.ts:213`) seeds each peer's own store with
itself. `handleRoute` (`:844–850`) checks, before choosing any hop, whether the deciding peer is
the successor or predecessor of the target coordinate in its own store; the globally-nearest peer
always is, so the route is recorded successful immediately.

Counterfactual run, confirming vacuity: forcing `selfCoord` to be supplied on *every* hop
(deleting the `hops > 0` split at `test/simulation/fret-sim.ts:887` — the exact behavior the case
is written to catch) leaves the case passing, 20/20 successes, hop counts all 0. Both runs were
made with a scratch harness driving the same scenario; the scratch file was deleted and the probe
edit reverted.

## What this ticket must decide and do

The case is worth having — the originator/forwarder split at `:887` is real production behavior
and nothing else pins it. It needs a scenario where the originator is *not* already an anchor in
its own store, so that the selector actually runs at hop 0 with no strict-improvement floor. Some
directions, none verified:

- Route from a peer that is near the target but not the nearest — one whose store names a closer
  peer — and assert the *first* hop is chosen at all (hops ≥ 1 and success), which is what the
  absent floor permits.
- Assert directly on the hop-0 selector call rather than through the route outcome.
- Or delete the case and state in the header that the split is unpinned, rather than leave a
  comment claiming a guard that is not there.

Whichever is chosen, the spec's header comment and the *Testing strategy* bullet in `docs/fret.md`
both currently assert that this case "pins the from-hop-2 floor". If the case is changed or
dropped, both statements must move with it — they are the reason this counts as a documentation
defect and not only a weak test.

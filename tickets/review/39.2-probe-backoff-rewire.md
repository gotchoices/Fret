## Correction to the source ticket's prediction

The ticket predicted the specs "will not compile". They **do** compile: every one of them reaches the
removed members through an `any` / structural cast, which is why `tsc --noEmit` is silent. The
breakage is therefore **runtime**, not compile-time, and `backoff-test-surface-migration` should
expect `TypeError: Cannot read properties of undefined` rather than TS errors.

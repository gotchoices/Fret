Phase 3 of the original `stabilize-tick-concurrency` work. The tick itself was pooled in that
ticket; this one gives the same treatment to the two connection warm-up passes, which were the last
serial dial chains left in maintenance.

## What changed

**One shared fan-out replaces two serial loops.** Both warm-up passes now call a new private
helper, `FretService.pingWarmupTargets(ids, label)` (`src/service/fret-service.ts`, just above
`preconnectNeighbors`):

- pooled through `runPooled` at `maintenanceConcurrency` (Core 6 / Edge 2) — the cap the
  stabilization tick already uses;
- against the **run signal**, not a tick deadline: neither pass runs inside `stabilizeOnce`, so
  there is no tick budget to inherit. The per-ping `MAINTENANCE_RPC_TIMEOUT_MS` (2000 ms) bounds
  each task, and `stop()` collapses the whole fan-out at once;
- `isDialable` filtered **before** the task list is built, so a pool slot goes to a peer that can
  actually be dialed rather than to a no-op;
- `diag.pingsSent` still increments only *after* a successful `sendPing`, unchanged;
- `wasCancelled(sig)` still guards each task's catch — these paths never scored anything, so all
  the guard suppresses is the log line;
- `logRejected` reused for the pool result (a rejection would be a bookkeeping bug, not peer
  evidence); its doc comment widened from "stabilization pool run" to cover maintenance generally.

Call sites: `preconnectNeighbors` (the start-up pass, up to `min(6, m)` per side → 12 targets) and
the tick body inside `startActivePreconnectLoop` (Core 6 / Edge 3 per second). Both keep their
unfiltered store walk and their outer try/catch; only the dial loop was replaced.

`sendAnnouncementsRateLimited` was **not** touched — it stays deliberately serial, as the prereq
ticket recorded in its comment.

## Test harness moved (bigger diff than the ticket implied)

`test/stabilize-concurrency.spec.ts` owned the stub-connection rig (`PeerRig`, `hangsUntilAbort`,
`stubStream`, plus `build` / `seedPeers` / `teardown` closures). The new warm-up tests need all of
it, so it was extracted to **`test/helpers/maintenance-rig.ts`** and both spec files import it.
`stabilize-concurrency.spec.ts` lost ~180 lines and gained a `buildMaintenanceRig` call; its eight
tests are otherwise unchanged and all still pass. **Reviewer: this refactor is the largest part of
the diff and was not asked for by the ticket** — the alternative was duplicating the rig into a
second file. Worth a look that nothing was silently altered in the move.

## Tests added — `test/preconnect-concurrency.spec.ts` (4 tests)

Every test drives `preconnectNeighbors()` directly (service never started, so `runSignal` is
`undefined`, which the pass accepts). 12 seeded peers is a full pass — the `min(6, m)` walk on each
side covers all 12 whatever their ring positions, so a test can pick which peers hang without
re-deriving the walk order.

- **`pings every reachable neighbor without waiting on the peers that never answer`** — 12 peers,
  **3** hang (`hangsUntilAbort`). Asserts the pass returns in ≤ 2000 + 1500 ms and ≥ 1000 ms, all 9
  reachable peers were pinged exactly once, and `pingsSent === 9`. Three hangs rather than one is
  what makes it non-vacuous: serially that is 3 × 2000 = 6000 ms and the assertion fails; pooled it
  measures ~2040 ms. **This is the only wall-clock assertion in the file** — see gaps below.
- **`Core: never more than 6 pings in flight, and 6 are reached`** / **`Edge: … 2 …`** — in-flight
  high-water mark asserted **equal** to `maintenanceConcurrency`, not merely at-most (at-most also
  passes for a serial walk). `rig.holdMs = 30` keeps overlapping opens open together.
- **`spends pool slots only on dialable peers`** — 6 of 12 peers made undialable (address withdrawn
  *and* stub connection withdrawn); asserts they are never opened, the other 6 still fill the
  6-wide pool, and `pingsSent === 6`. This is what pins the "filter before the task list" rule.

## Known gaps — treat these as the floor, not the finish line

- **The active preconnect tick body is not directly tested.** Both passes funnel through
  `pingWarmupTargets`, and the tests drive the wider of the two (`preconnectNeighbors`), so the
  pooling behaviour is covered transitively — but nothing asserts that the active tick's own
  `slice(0, budget)` target selection, its `preconnectGen` re-arm guard, or its interaction with
  `setMode('active')` survived the edit. A test that drives `startActivePreconnectLoop` through one
  tick would close this.
- **One wall-clock assertion.** The headline test's 2000 + 1500 ms bound is the only timing-based
  assertion; it measured ~2040 ms locally with wide slack, but it is the kind of assertion that
  goes flaky on a loaded CI box. A deterministic alternative (asserting completion *ordering*
  rather than elapsed time) would be sturdier and was not attempted.
- **No test for `stop()` landing mid-pass.** `dead-state.spec.ts` covers "already cancelled before
  the pass starts" (0 dials). Nothing covers a signal that aborts while tasks are in flight, where
  the interesting behaviour is that started tasks abort and unstarted ones are `skipped` — the
  stabilization tick has that test, the warm-up passes do not.
- **The harness move.** See above; the mechanical extraction is unreviewed except by the tests
  passing.
- **`pingWarmupTargets` takes a `label` string used only for log formatting.** It is the one seam
  where the two call sites still differ; a mismatched label would be invisible to every test.

## What changed

**`event-scheduler.ts:3`** — `'churn'` added to the `SimEvent['type']` union. No other scheduler
change; the array re-sort per insert is stable, so same-timestamp churn and stabilize events keep
insertion order.

**`fret-sim.ts`**
- `scheduleChurn()` now emits bare `{ type: 'churn' }` events on the same cadence
  (`t = intervalMs; t < durationMs; t += intervalMs`) and picks nobody. It previously drew every
  leaver from the *initial* population at setup time, so a late joiner could never churn and a
  peer drawn twice produced a second no-op leave.
- New `handleChurn()` runs at fire time: `rng.pick` over the *currently* alive peers →
  `handleLeave(id)` → `handleJoin()`. Late joiners are eligible, duplicate picks are impossible,
  and the population is stationary.
- `handleEvent` switch gained `case 'churn'`.
- `SimConfig.churnRatePerSec` doc comment now states the pairing explicitly (departure + arrival
  per event) and why leave-only churn made every non-zero-rate threshold a measurement of
  population collapse.

**`simulation.spec.ts`** — the two `totalJoins !== n` throws on churn-enabled cases (N=25 @ 0.25/s,
N=100 @ 5/s) relaxed to `< n`, since `totalJoins` now exceeds `n` by the churn-event count. The two
N=5 / N=10 cases run `churnRatePerSec: 0` and were left exact. Suite timeout raised 60s → 180s with
a `NOTE:` giving the reason and the measured number (see *Cost* below).

**`churn-scenarios.spec.ts`** — two new cases:
- *continuous churn keeps the population stationary* — N=40 @ 2/s for 10s; asserts
  `totalLeaves === churnEvents`, `totalJoins === n + churnEvents`, and `aliveCount() === n`. This
  is the case that would have failed outright before the change (the run ended at 20 alive).
- *churn replays identically across runs at one seed* — N=30 @ 3/s, two runs, `JSON.stringify`
  compare of the metrics, plus a non-vacuity guard (`totalLeaves > 0`). Lazy scheduling is what
  could have introduced order-dependence; this pins that it did not.

## The ticket was wrong about two files — verified, no change needed

The ticket flagged `message-bus.spec.ts:166` and `churn-scenarios.spec.ts:209` as suspected
`totalJoins` sites needing the same relaxation. Both were read. Neither line is an assertion — they
are `churnRatePerSec:` config lines. Grepping `totalJoins|totalLeaves` across `test/` gives the
complete set of assertion sites; the only ones on a churn-enabled config are the two in
`simulation.spec.ts` that were relaxed. `message-bus.spec.ts:418`, `churn-scenarios.spec.ts:94` and
`sim-profiles.spec.ts:54,67` all sit on `churnRatePerSec: 0` configs and stay exact.
`message-bus.spec.ts` is therefore untouched by this ticket.

## What a reviewer should push on

- **Threshold re-calibration was not needed, and that is worth double-checking.** RNG draws moved
  from setup time to fire time, so every seeded run's numbers shifted, and the ticket predicted the
  churn suites might need new thresholds. They did not: `routing under churn` (the only pre-existing
  churn-enabled case in `churn-scenarios.spec.ts`) came in at 95.0% success / 0.9 avg hops against
  its ≥80% and ≤7.6 bars — comfortable margins, so nothing was re-set. No threshold constant in any
  touched suite was changed. If a reviewer thinks a margin is now thinner than it reads, the numbers
  above are the ones to compare against.
- **The two new cases use hard equality on counts, not margins.** `churnEvents` is recomputed in the
  test as `floor(durationMs / floor(1000 / rate)) - 1`, duplicating `scheduleChurn`'s own loop
  arithmetic. That is deliberate (a margin would not catch an off-by-one in the pairing) but it
  means a future change to the churn cadence breaks these two tests by construction. Judge whether
  that is the right coupling.
- **Cost.** Holding the population at 100 instead of letting it decay toward 50 roughly doubles the
  N=100 case's per-tick stabilization work. Measured: 27.4s in isolation. The suite timeout was
  raised rather than the case shortened, because shortening `durationMs` is a scope call, not an
  implementation one — the `NOTE:` at the timeout says to shorten `durationMs` (not lower
  `churnRatePerSec`) if the wall time ever becomes a problem, since the rate is what the case
  measures. Reviewer may prefer to make that cut now.
- **Departed peers are never reclaimed.** `handleLeave` sets `alive = false` but leaves the peer in
  `this.peers` and its store in `this.stores`; churn therefore grows both maps for the run's
  duration (149 entries at the end of the N=100 run). Every hot path filters on `alive`, so this is
  memory, not per-tick cost. Pre-existing behavior that this change makes more visible, not a
  regression introduced here — flagging it rather than fixing it inside this ticket.
- Un-exercised: `handleChurn` when the alive population is empty. `rng.pick` returns `undefined` and
  the code falls through to `handleJoin()`, which repopulates. No test drives it; reachable only by
  a batch-leave draining the ring while churn is scheduled.

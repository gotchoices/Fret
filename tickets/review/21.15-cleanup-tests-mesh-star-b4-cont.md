  specs adopt it ... add a `starMesh(count, opts)` convenience here". Counting with
  `grep -rn "addServices" packages/fret/test` found **five** call sites, four of them the identical

  `addServices` and is deliberately left alone. Condition tripped, so the extraction was taken

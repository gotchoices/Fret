## Already done (run 3 — verified, do not redo)

- **Thread 1 (two clocks) — fixed inline, in the working tree, uncommitted.**
  `recordContactSuccess` scored at an injected `now()` (`scheduler.getCurrentTime()`) while
  `recordContactFailure` scored at its `time` argument. Those agree inside `run()`'s loop but not
  on the scheduler's two other public paths: `processEvent(evt)` calls `handleEvent` directly and
  never touches `currentTime`, and `advanceTo(t)` sets `currentTime` to the *target* rather than
  the last fired event's time. Fix applied: `recordContactSuccess` takes a `time` parameter, both
  call sites (`contactSweep`, `handleRoute`) pass it, and the `now` dep is gone from
  `LivenessDeps`, from the private field, and from the `fret-sim.ts` deps literal.
- **Verified after that edit:** `npx tsc --noEmit` clean, and
  `simulation.partition.spec.ts` + `simulation.routing.spec.ts` both green (13 passing, 46s) —
  including the deterministic-replay case, which is the pin for this class of change.

## Verified facts (do not re-check)

- `src/store/relevance.ts` was consumed as-is; it is not in the changeset.
- Omitting `avgLatencyMs` from the success patch is **correct**, not an oversight. `recordSuccess`'s
  own doc states a caller supplying no sample must omit the field from any derived patch. The
  implementer passes `latencyMs: undefined` and omits it, so `avgLatencyMs` stays `null` and takes
  the neutral 0.5 penalty — the documented behavior in `docs/fret.md` (*Unmeasured latency is
  `null`, not `0`*).
- `lastAccess: time` in the failure patch is redundant (`recordFailure` already returns
  `lastAccess: time`) but harmless. Left as-is deliberately: the comment above it explains the
  sim-clock rule, and collapsing it to `scored.lastAccess` buys nothing.
- **Thread 4 — the per-tick whole-store scoring magnitude is already parked. Do not re-file.**
  `contactSweep` scores every reachable non-dead entry every tick, so `accessCount` and KDE
  occupancy grow by one per entry per peer per tick. There is a `NOTE:` at `recordContactSuccess`
  recording it as a tripwire with a revisit condition ("if a suite ever needs frequency to
  discriminate, give the sweep its own cadence"). That reads adequately; nothing more to do.

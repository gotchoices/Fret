## What changed (all in `packages/fret/src/service/fret-service.ts`)

**Run-scoped cancellation.**
- New field `runAbort: AbortController | null`, minted in `start()` immediately after
  `this.runGen++`, aborted + nulled in `stop()` immediately after `this.clearLoopTimers()` —
  i.e. *before* `removeNodeListeners()`, `unregisterRpcHandlers()` and the leave fan-out. A
  start→stop→start cycle mints a fresh controller (mirrors the `runGen` placement).
- `private get runSignal(): AbortSignal | undefined` — `undefined` between runs.
- `private wasCancelled(sig): boolean` — `sig?.aborted === true`. Doc-comment explains why the
  *caller's* signal (not the sender's child deadline) is the discriminator, and why every call
  site captures it in a local before its loop / `try`.

**New constants beside the other `private static readonly`s:**
- `MAINTENANCE_RPC_TIMEOUT_MS = 2000` — whole-RPC budget for ping and announce (single-hop link
  RPCs). Named this rather than the ticket's suggested `MAINTENANCE_PING_TIMEOUT_MS` because it
  covers announce too; doc-comment states why `sendMaybeAct` keeps the 5 s default (route budget,
  not link budget).
- `SHUTDOWN_BUDGET_MS = 3000` — cap on the whole leave fan-out inside `stop()`.
- `LEAVE_NOTICE_TIMEOUT_MS = 1500` — per-notice budget inside that cap.

## Known gaps / what the reviewer should press on

- **No test yet asserts the crux**: that a `stop()` racing an in-flight ping/fetch/forward
  records no `pingsFail`, no contact strike, no backoff. That is 7.3's job — the code paths are
  in place, but only type-checked and exercised indirectly. Suggested shape for 7.3: hang a
  peer's ping handler (never reply), start a stabilization tick, `stop()` mid-flight, assert
  `contactFailures` and `diag.pingsFail` unchanged for that peer.
- **`stop()` ordering is asserted only indirectly.** `churn.leave.spec.ts` still sees leave
  notices arrive, which proves the fan-out is not silenced by the abort — but no test pins that
  the abort fires *before* the fan-out or that the fan-out honours `SHUTDOWN_BUDGET_MS`.
- **`iterativeLookup` under cancellation** — on abort the walk `break`s to `exhausted`. If the
  activity resend was the thing cancelled, the activity was not delivered and the caller sees
  `exhausted`, not an error. That matches the ticket's stated design (cancellation is not an
  error about the peer), but is worth a second look for whether callers want to distinguish
  "exhausted" from "cancelled by stop()".
- **`routeAct` forward under cancellation** returns a `NearAnchor` (built with real anchors,
  since it is post-token, per the existing rule). The inbound stream is being torn down at that
  point anyway; harmless but noted.
- Timer hygiene: the only new `deadline()` the service itself builds is in
  `sendLeaveToNeighbors`, and it is cancelled in a `finally`. All other budgets are built (and
  cancelled) inside the RPC senders. Verified by the mocha exit watchdog not firing across the
  138 tests above.

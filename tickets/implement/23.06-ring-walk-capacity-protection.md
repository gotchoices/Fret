First of three tickets split out of the former `implement/23.05-ring-walk-migrate-call-sites`, which

any of the three has been implemented. The other two are `23.07-ring-walk-leave-path` and
`23.08-ring-walk-remaining-sites`, in that order.

The shared helper already exists and is already spec'd — `ringNeighborsBothSides` in
`packages/fret/src/service/ring-walk.ts`, with `test/ring-walk.spec.ts` beside it. Read its doc
comment first: it states the contract (`count` means *peers besides self*; each side is over-fetched
by `1 + exclude.size` and trimmed; the result is **side-interleaved**, s1, p1, s2, p2, …). This
ticket assumes that contract and does not restate it.

## Root cause

A ring walk anchored exactly on a stored coordinate returns that entry as its own first result on
**both** sides. Every self-anchored copy of the two-sided walk in the service asks each side for `m`
and then filters self out of the concatenation, so it keeps only `m - 1` peers per side. For
`enforceCapacity` that means the m-th successor and the m-th predecessor — genuine members of the
successor/predecessor window — are evictable when the table is at capacity.

## Behavior this lands

`enforceCapacity` protects `2 * max(2, m) + 1` ids (self + m per side) instead of today's
`2 * max(2, m) - 1`. The m-th successor and m-th predecessor are no longer evictable at capacity.
A store at capacity therefore has two fewer eviction candidates than before.

`DigitreeStore.protectedIdsAround` is **deleted**, not fixed. It is a two-sided walk with exactly
one caller (`enforceCapacity`); leaving it in place means the over-fetch rule lives in two modules.

## Edge cases this ticket owns

- **Ring of exactly one peer (self).** The helper returns empty; `enforceCapacity` must still
  protect self. Adding self to the returned set is what covers this — do not rely on the walk.
- **Protection still outranks the cap.** With the widened set the pathological condition becomes
  `capacity < 2m + 1` rather than `capacity < 2m - 1`. Keep that bullet, adjust its arithmetic.
  Unreachable at the shipped numbers (m 8, capacity 2048).

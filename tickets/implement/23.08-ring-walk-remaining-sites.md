The contract that matters most here: `count` means *peers besides self*, and the result is

### Notes per site

- **`announceTargetsAround` slices per side now.** Its `.slice(0, fanout)` runs over the
  interleaved union, so a Core fanout of 8 against `m = 8` stops being successor-only. The
  non-connected-first re-sort stays; pass the existing `exclude` set through as the helper's
  `exclude` (self is excluded by the helper regardless).
- **`isNearNeighbor` reads the nullable `this.cachedSelfCoord` and returns `false` when unset** —
  it is synchronous and must stay synchronous. Keep that guard.
- **`windowGaps` drops its local `+ 1`** and calls the helper at `m`, keeping its own `offsets` seed
  of `0n` for self. Its explanatory comment about asking each side for `m + 1` goes with it.
- **`warmupTargetIds`** is `min(radius, m)` per side, self-filtered; callers pass `radius` 6
  (start-up pass) and 12 (active tick).

## Deliberately out of scope — `getNeighbors` (`fret-service.ts` ~2542)

Not migrated, on purpose. It is on the public `FretService` interface, key-anchored rather than
self-anchored (so the off-by-one does not apply — no entry normally sits on a key's coordinate), it
does **not** exclude self (self is a legitimate neighbor of a key), and its `wants` is both the
per-side count and the total cap. Folding it in would change public behavior to fix a defect it does
not have. Leave a `NOTE:` at the site recording that its concatenate-then-slice is successor-biased
by the design of the `wants` contract, and that the helper deliberately does not cover it.

Also **not** call sites of this idiom, and to be left alone: `assembleCohort`
(`src/service/cohort.ts` ~39, the key-anchored alternating cohort walk with its own over-fetch rule)
and the simulation harness / measurement code under `test/simulation/`, which walks the store
directly with its own `notDead` filter.

## Test that will break, and why — read before running

`test/preconnect-concurrency.spec.ts` ~180-184 rebuilds `warmupTargetIds`' walk **by hand** to
compute its expected target list:

```
const walk = Array.from(new Set([
    ...harness.store.neighborsRight(selfCoord, Math.min(12, m)),
    ...harness.store.neighborsLeft(selfCoord, Math.min(12, m))
]))
```

It then asserts on the *contents and ordering* of that list
(`expect(dialed).to.deep.equal(walk.slice(ACTIVE_BUDGET, ACTIVE_BUDGET * 2))`), and it blocks
`walk.slice(0, ACTIVE_BUDGET)` to choose ring positions deterministically. Two differences from the
migrated `warmupTargetIds` will bite: the hand-rolled copy is **side-major** where the helper is
interleaved, and it does **not** filter self where `warmupTargetIds` does. Point the test at
`ringNeighborsBothSides` rather than restating the walk — this is the seventh copy of the idiom and
the reason it must not stay a copy.

## Background

The `framed-read-false-truncation` fix landed. `readFramed` now has two implementations behind one
signature, dispatched by `isMessageStream` (`packages/fret/src/rpc/protocols.ts` ~line 350):

- **libp2p stream** -> `byteStream` from `@libp2p/utils`, reading frames at the same layer as the
  stream's own EOF state. No timer, no poll.
- **plain async iterable** (test stubs, non-libp2p sources) -> the `lp.decode` path, ending on
  iterator EOF alone.

The 20 ms end-of-stream poll and its `EOF_POLL_MS` constant are **deleted**. Several documents and
comments still describe them as current. Every site below was located by grep this run; the list is
believed complete for `EOF_POLL_MS` and "20 ms poll".

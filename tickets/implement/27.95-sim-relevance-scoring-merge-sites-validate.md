## The deviation from production, recorded in code but worth repeating here

The sim's kernel density estimate is fed by gossip, and an already-held entry is re-scored on
every merge. Production does neither (a gossiped peer is scored once at creation and its distance
never observed). The reason: a sim peer sees orders of magnitude fewer contact events than a real
node, so proven-contact-only feeding supplies at most about `maxConnections` observations per
peer — at `alpha = 0.03` occupancy stays near 0, the ideal/density ratio stays above
`sMax^(1/beta) ~= 2.63`, and every entry clamps at `sMax = 1.8` and ties. That is the same
degeneracy relevance ranking exists to escape, one layer down. The `NOTE:` lives on `scoreMerge`
with one-line pointers at the merge sites.

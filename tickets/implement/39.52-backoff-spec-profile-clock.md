## Watch for

**`factor(id) === 0` is the honest replacement for `map.get(id) === undefined`.** A retained but
window-closed entry reports `factor > 0`, so the two differ exactly where the retention rule
matters.

**Don't reintroduce a second cast** to reach `ProbeBackoff` internals. The public surface is
constructor `{ capacity, baseMs?, maxFactor?, retainMs?, now? }`; readonly `baseMs`, `maxFactor`,
`retainMs`, `capacity`; getter `size`; methods `record`, `clear`, `clearAll`, `isBackedOff`,
`penalty`, `factor`, `sweep`, `prune`; statics `DEFAULT_BASE_MS = 1000`,
`DEFAULT_MAX_FACTOR = 32`, `DEFAULT_RETAIN_MS = 300_000`. If a spec wants something not on that
list, raise it in the handoff rather than casting around it.

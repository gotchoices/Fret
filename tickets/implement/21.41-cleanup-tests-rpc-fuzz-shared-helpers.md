## Why this is its own step

`packages/fret/test/rpc.handler-fuzz.spec.ts` is 2115 lines (pre-edit) holding three tiers of
tests. Two of those tiers are moving out into their own files. Both moved tiers and the tier that
stays use the same small fixtures at the top of the file, so the fixtures have to live somewhere
all three can import from *before* the move, or the move turns into "relocate a block and
hand-copy its fixtures" — which is how three copies of one rule drift apart.

Doing it as a separate step also means the move that follows is provably a pure move: this ticket
changes no test, adds no test, and deletes no test.

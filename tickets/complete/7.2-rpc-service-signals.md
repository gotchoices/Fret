## What shipped (all in `packages/fret/src/service/fret-service.ts`)

**Run-scoped cancellation.** `runAbort: AbortController` minted in `start()` beside `runGen++`,
aborted in `stop()` immediately after `clearLoopTimers()` — before `removeNodeListeners()`,
`unregisterRpcHandlers()` and the leave fan-out. `private get runSignal` exposes it;
`private wasCancelled(sig)` is the discriminator every call site checks in its `catch` before
scoring anything.

**Constants:** `MAINTENANCE_RPC_TIMEOUT_MS = 2000` (ping + announce — single-hop link budget),
`SHUTDOWN_BUDGET_MS = 3000` (whole leave fan-out), `LEAVE_NOTICE_TIMEOUT_MS = 1500` (per notice).
`sendMaybeAct` and `fetchNeighbors` keep the 5 s default — those are route/payload budgets.

**Call sites**, all with `{ signal, ... }` on the send and a `wasCancelled` guard ahead of every
`noteRpcFailure` / `recordBackoff` / `diag.pingsFail++`: `sendAnnouncementsRateLimited`,
`preconnectNeighbors`, the active-preconnect tick, `probeNeighborsLatency`, `probeMembership`,
`mergeNeighborSnapshots`, the `routeAct` forward, and `iterativeLookup` (probe + activity resend +
outer hop catch). `sendLeaveToNeighbors` is the deliberate exception: it runs *after* the abort on
its own `deadline(SHUTDOWN_BUDGET_MS)`, cancelled in a `finally`, with an `aborted` check at the
top of both loops.

## Review findings

**Fixed in this pass (1)**

- **`stop()` discarded the aborted controller (`this.runAbort = null`), which turned "cancelled"
  back into "no signal" for every call site that reads `runSignal` after the abort — and several
  do.** `stabilizeOnce` has no `stopped` re-check between its five passes, and each pass captures
  `this.runSignal` itself, so a `stop()` landing during `seedFromPeerStore()` / `seedFromBootstraps()`
  left `probeNeighborsLatency`, `mergeNeighborSnapshots` and `probeMembership` running with
  `sig === undefined`: they dialed after teardown, held shutdown open for their full 2 s/5 s budget,
  and recorded contact strikes and backoff against healthy peers. That is precisely the failure the
  ticket exists to prevent, merely narrowed from "the whole tick" to "whatever ran after the abort".
  Same hole for `iterativeLookup` / `routeAct` reached after `stop()`. Fix: keep the aborted
  controller and let the next `start()` replace it (it already mints a fresh one unconditionally);
  `null` now means only "never started". Doc comments on the field, the getter, and the `stop()`
  site updated to state the invariant and why. Also makes `7.5-stabilize-tick-concurrency`'s stated
  assumption — "the tick deadline is a child of the run signal, so `stop()` collapses the whole
  tick" — actually true regardless of when the tick opens its deadline.

**Checked and correct — no finding**

- Guard order at every `catch` in the table: the `wasCancelled` check precedes *all* scoring
  (`noteRpcFailure`, `recordBackoff`, `applyFailure`, `diag.pingsFail++`) at all nine sites.
- No call site re-reads `this.runSignal` inside its loop; every one captures a local first, so a
  mid-loop `stop()`+`start()` cannot swap the signal being compared.
- Control flow after cancellation is right at each site: `return` where the pass is done,
  `break` where post-loop cleanup must still run (the active-preconnect tick's `release()`,
  `mergeNeighborSnapshots`'s `enforceCapacity`, `iterativeLookup`'s trailing `exhausted` yield).
  `break` inside `iterativeLookup`'s nested `try` does exit the attempt loop, as intended.
- Every outbound RPC in `src/` is covered — a grep of `sendPing|sendMaybeAct|fetchNeighbors|
  announceNeighbors|sendLeave` finds no unsignalled call site left.
- Timer hygiene: the only `deadline()` the service builds is `sendLeaveToNeighbors`'s, cancelled in
  a `finally`; every other budget is built and cancelled inside the RPC senders. The mocha exit
  watchdog did not fire across the full 616-test run.
- Abort-listener accumulation on the long-lived run signal: each `deadline(ms, runSignal)` attaches
  one listener and removes it in `cancel()`, and `readAllBounded` listens on the child, not the
  parent. Measured Node 24 raises no `MaxListenersExceededWarning` for plain `AbortSignal`
  listeners, so the Core 128-inbound-stream ceiling is not a noise source. Not a concern.
- `stop()` still delivers leave notices after the abort (own budget, no parent) — `churn.leave`
  passes unchanged.

**Deliberately not changed**

- The four repetitions of `{ signal: sig, timeoutMs: FretService.MAINTENANCE_RPC_TIMEOUT_MS }` are
  left inline rather than folded into a helper. A getter is the obvious DRY move and is the *wrong*
  one — it would re-read `runSignal` per send, defeating the capture-once invariant the guard
  depends on; a helper taking `sig` saves one line at four sites and adds indirection over the one
  thing a reader most needs to see literally.
- `routeAct`'s cancelled forward returns a real `NearAnchor` (two ring walks). Local work only, on
  a stream already being torn down. Not worth a branch.

## Outcome

A usable gap exists at `capacity: 32` on a 1000-peer all-edge ring. Shipped `minDistance`: 100%
success on all four seeds, p90 7–8 hops. Clockwise-only: 83–90% success, p90 17–21. XOR: 60–77%
success, p90 5–7. The load-bearing finding is that **XOR's p90 is lower than the shipped
metric's**, because p90 is taken over successful routes only and XOR fails the hard routes rather
than lengthening them — so a hop bound catches clockwise-only and is vacuous for XOR, and the
success floor is what catches XOR. Recommendation handed to `28.6`: cap 32, assert both
`success >= 0.95` and `p90 <= 12`.

## Review findings

**Checked:** the implement diff (`baa1e8e`) with fresh eyes before the handoff summary; working
tree state (`git status`, `git diff` over `packages/fret/src` and `packages/fret/test`); every
factual claim the caveat and the reproduction recipe make, against the code they name
(`minDistance` readers, `chooseNextHop`'s direct import, `normalizedLogDistance`,
`nearRadiusFor`, `measureRouting`, `successfulRouteHops`, the `capacity` knob, the sim's
relevance-scoring sites); internal consistency of the 3×3 sweep against the four-seed table and
against the recommendation table.

**Fixed inline (minor):**

- The caveat claimed the sim's `nearRadiusFor` reads `minDistance`. It does not — it is pure ring
  arithmetic (`2^256 / store.size() * k * 2`, `fret-sim.ts:879`) and calls no distance function;
  nothing under `test/simulation/` references `minDistance` at all. What a substitution actually
  changes there is the meaning of the selector's comparison *against* that radius. The caveat's
  other arm (the relevance sparsity model, via `normalizedLogDistance`) is correct and is now
  cited to its call sites. Left uncorrected, a downstream reader would over-attribute the
  eviction-shape contamination.
- The recommendation table read `5 below substituted best` on the p90 row. Substituted *best* at
  cap 32 is XOR's 5, which is below the recommended bound, not above it; the 17 is clockwise-only's
  worst. Reworded to `5 below clockwise-only's best`, and a sentence added above the table saying
  the `worst substituted` column is per-axis and that the hop bound is deliberately vacuous for
  XOR. The finding section already said this in prose — the table contradicted it.

**Major — none filed.** No new tickets. The one architectural gap the ticket surfaces (there is no
injection seam for the ring metric, so a substitution must edit `src/ring/distance.ts` and
therefore also perturbs eviction) is already stated in `28.6`'s caveat as a separate concern, and
filing it now would pre-empt `28.6`'s own decision about whether the guard needs isolation at all.

**Tripwires — none recorded.** The known gaps below are stated limits of a completed measurement,
not conditional future work, so there is no site to park a `NOTE:` at.

**Lint + tests — not run, and this is not a skipped step.** There is no lint step in this repo
(`yarn check` is the gate; `yarn format` is documented as not-to-be-run). No source or test file
changed in this ticket's diff or in this review's, so there is nothing for the suite to exercise
that `28.6` will not exercise when it lands the guard. The two edits made here are prose inside a
ticket file.

**Reproducibility — verified by reading, not by re-running.** The recipe in `28.6`'s caption is
complete and every symbol it names exists as described. The numbers themselves were **not**
independently re-measured: this review hit its token budget before spending the ~2.5 minutes of
wall time plus two hand edits to `src/ring/distance.ts` that a re-run costs. The shipped-baseline
cells (no source edit needed) are the cheapest spot-check if anyone wants one.

## Known gaps carried forward from the measurement

- Single-run cells; seed spread across four seeds at caps 28 and 32 is the only variance estimate.
  Cap 24 was measured at seed 4242 alone and must not be quoted as a range.
- The `success >= 0.95` floor has ~5 points of margin on each side and is the tightest number in
  the recommendation. If `28.6` sees the shipped case dip below 100% on an untested seed, the floor
  is what to re-examine — the hop bound has ~50% headroom.
- The `capacity < 2m + 1` protection interaction was not investigated. At `m: 8` the protection set
  is 17 ids against caps of 24–32, so eviction has 7–15 unprotected slots; the recommended cap of
  32 should not be lowered without re-measuring.

## Before you build optimystic

1. **`xorDistance` is gone and optimystic imports it.**
   `packages/substrate-simulator/src/ring-model.ts:1` and
   `packages/substrate-simulator/test/fret-model.spec.ts:2` both import it by name. The
   ring-distance consolidation removed it in favour of `minDistance`, deliberately — XOR maximizes
   where arc length minimizes, so it was giving that simulator wrong answers near the wrap-around
   seam. **This is optimystic's edit, not FRET's**; re-adding the export would keep the wrong metric
   in use. It is a behavior change, not only a rename — the numbers move.
2. **Resolution is mixed today, which is why it has not bitten yet.** optimystic's root
   `package.json` uses `p2p-fret: portal:../Fret/packages/fret` (this working tree, live), while
   `packages/substrate-simulator/package.json` pins `^0.6.0` from npm. `^0.6.0` will not accept
   0.7.0, so the moment you bump that pin, the import breaks.
3. **`openRpcStream` now ships**, which was the thing optimystic asked for. Its
   `debt-shared-limited-connection-dial-options` ticket named exporting it as the preferred fix and
   recorded that it was "gated on a dependency release" — that gate is open. `releaseRpcStream`
   ships alongside it as its mandatory partner (aborts once the caller's signal fired, `close()`s
   otherwise, because `close()` against a stalled remote is unbounded). Three hand-written copies of
   that connection selection can now be deleted downstream, including the broken one at
   `libp2p-node-base.ts:1041` that omits `runOnLimitedConnection` and silently drops its
   `AbortOptions`.
4. **Metadata is now `Record<string, unknown>`, not `Record<string, any>`** — consumers must narrow
   before use. Landed deliberately in the same release as the removals above, so the break happens
   once rather than twice.

Full breaking-change list is in `.release-notes.pending.md` — five items, verified by diffing
`src/index.ts` against the `v0.6.0` tag rather than from memory.

`concurrency-inflight-tests`.

**One real defect, found by the fuzzing:** `rpc-handler-fault-isolation` — inbound RPC handlers
leaked their connection slot permanently when a malformed or misbehaving message made them fail
partway through, leaving the stream neither closed nor aborted. Now every inbound handler goes
through one registration seam (`registerRpcHandler`) that closes a handler-left-open stream on
success and aborts on error, so the leak is unrepresentable per-handler rather than patched at five
call sites.

**Also filed, not fixed:** `backlog/debt-rejection-diagnostics-conflated` — `diag.rejected.rateLimited`
turns out to be shared by **six** distinct causes across five protocols, so the counter cannot tell
you why anything was rejected. Filed at the representation rung rather than as a point fix.

framing migration deleted `readAllBounded` *and* the 20 ms EOF poll that helper deliberately
carried, without landing the subscribe-before-writing priming meant to replace it. Consequence,
confirmed by repro rather than reasoning: a fast responder's FIN is processed while the dialer is

**silently, with no event**. `lp.decode`'s pending `next()` never settles and the read burns the
whole 5 s deadline. Two real-transport tests in `rpc.stream-errors.spec.ts` failed on it. The poll
is restored inside `readFramed`.

## A scheduling trap worth knowing about

`15.2b-rpc-sender-migration` sat in `implement/` across four live re-ranks while the tree stayed
red at typecheck, and it was not a runner bug. **A filename like `15.2b-` carries no valid numeric
sequence prefix** — the trailing letter breaks the parse — so tess treats the ticket as
*unnumbered*, and unnumbered tickets sort after every numbered one in the same stage. Its siblings
`15.31`/`15.32`/`15.33` parse fine, so they outranked it on every pass. Same trap applies to the
`15.2a1` / `15.2a3` style names.

It was renamed to `15.2-` (numeric, and correctly ahead of the 15.3x chain) and picked up
immediately. **Agents splitting a ticket should suffix with a decimal (`15.21`), never a letter.**
Worth watching for in the review of the tickets that used the letter form.

## Note on the runner's budget

`--token-budget 120000` repeatedly stalls an agent mid-discovery: it writes its findings into the
ticket and exits without editing, and the next run does the edit. That costs roughly double on the
stalled runs. **Left in place deliberately.** The stalls are what force these tickets to
decompose, the resulting per-file handoff notes are accurate, and one of those investigation-only
runs is what found the half-close regression instead of shipping it. A larger budget would more
likely land a big ticket as one half-finished edit than as clean slices.
